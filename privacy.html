Privacy Policy

Sybyke sticker journal app

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sybyke app ("Sybyke", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Sybyke lets you combine photos, stickers, text and drawings into journal pages and render them as images. We designed it so your journal stays on your device.

At a glance
  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • Your pages, photos, text and drawings are stored only on your device. We never upload them.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics or profiling software.
  • Device permissions are requested only when you use the feature that needs them.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Sybyke app on iPhone and iPad and to the support and legal pages that the app opens. It does not cover services run by other companies, such as the App Store, iCloud or your photo library. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information you create in the app

When you use Sybyke, you may create or add the following content. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

  • Journal pages and drafts: the template, background, sticker positions, text, fonts, colors and drawings that make up each page.
  • Photos: copies of the photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library to place on a page. Your originals in the Photos app are never changed or deleted.
  • Rendered pages: the finished page images you create with Render Page.
  • Dictated text: text produced by Dictate, which you review before adding it to a page.

We have no access to this content. It is never sent to us or to any server we control.

3. Information stored for the app to work

  • Page Credits and activity: your credit balance, plus a local list of welcome credits, purchases and renders. Purchase entries include the transaction identifier Apple returns, so the same purchase is never counted twice.
  • Preferences: simple settings, such as whether you have finished the welcome screens and whether the one-time system prompts on Home have been shown.

This information also stays on your device.

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, address or contacts.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or social media SDKs.
  • We do not read your photo library beyond the photos you choose.
  • We do not record or keep audio.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymized crash and usage statistics. Apple controls this under its own privacy policy, and it does not identify you personally.

5. Device permissions

Each permission uses the standard iOS system prompt and is requested only when needed. You can change your choices at any time in the iOS Settings app. Declining a permission never blocks the rest of the app.

6. Speech recognition

Dictate uses Apple's Speech framework. If your device supports on-device recognition, your speech is processed entirely on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers to return a transcription, under the Apple Privacy Policy. Sybyke receives only the resulting text. It does not store or transmit audio, and you can edit or discard the text before it is added to a page.

7. In-app purchases

Page Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment method, billing details and receipts. Sybyke receives only the information it needs to deliver your credits, such as the product purchased and the transaction identifier. Sybyke checks Apple's transaction verification on your device before adding credits. If the app closes during a purchase, the verified purchase is delivered automatically the next time you open Sybyke.

8. Advertising and tracking

Sybyke contains no advertising. It does not request permission to track you, does not access the advertising identifier, does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and does not share data with data brokers.

9. Notifications

Sybyke shows the system notification prompt once but does not send local or push notifications, whatever you choose. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

10. Third-party components

Sybyke is built with Apple's frameworks and a few open-source user interface libraries (for layout, keyboard handling, progress indicators and short messages). These libraries run entirely on your device and do not collect or transmit information.

11. Support and legal pages

This policy, the Terms of Use and the Support page are hosted on GitHub Pages and open inside the app. When any web page loads, the hosting provider may record standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, for security and reliability. This is handled under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages use no cookies or analytics from us.

12. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, because the app does not send it to us. Your content leaves the app only when you choose to, for example by saving a rendered page to your photo library and sharing it from there.

13. Data storage and security

Your content is stored in the app's sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read. It is protected by the data protection features of iOS, which work best when your device has a passcode. Your device's own backup settings may include app data in iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you.

14. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete a single rendered page from its detail screen.
  • Discard a draft when you leave the editor.
  • Erase everything with Settings → Clear All Data. This removes drafts, rendered pages, copied photos, Page Credits, the activity list and preferences.
  • Delete the app, which removes all of its data from the device.

Images you have already saved to your photo library, and the original photos in your library, are not affected by any of these actions.

15. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. These may include the right to access, correct, delete or move it, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Examples are the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act. Because Sybyke keeps your data only on your device, you can use these rights directly in the app: view and edit your pages, delete individual items, or clear all data. We do not hold copies we could provide or delete for you. You can manage permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

16. Children's privacy

Sybyke is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not collect personal information on any server. If you are a parent or guardian, you can review and delete the app's content directly on the device.

17. International users

Sybyke can be used worldwide. Your journal content is not transferred to us in any country, because it never leaves your device through the app. Services you use separately, such as the App Store and Apple speech recognition, may process data in other countries under Apple's policies.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add features or when laws change. The current version is always available on this page and from Settings in the app. If we make a significant change to how information is handled, we will also describe it in the app's release notes before the change takes effect.

19. Contact

If you have questions or requests about this policy, please visit the Sybyke Support page or use the support contact listed on Sybyke's App Store page.
