Sybyke Support

Answers to common questions about Sybyke, the sticker journal app.

How do I make a page?

Tap the + button and pick a template. Add photos with the camera or from Photos, or use the sample photos. Decorate the page with stickers, text and drawings, then tap Review and Render Page.

What is a Page Credit?

Rendering a finished page uses 1 Page Credit. Editing and saving drafts is always free. A credit is used only when the render saves successfully.

I bought credits but they did not appear.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. This finishes any purchase Apple approved that has not been delivered yet. Purchases waiting for approval, such as Ask to Buy, are added automatically once they are approved.

Can I move my pages to a new device?

Pages are stored only on the device where you made them. Use Save to Photos to keep a copy of each page in your photo library.

Dictate or the camera is not working.

Open the iOS Settings app, find Sybyke, and allow Camera, Microphone, Speech Recognition or Photos as needed. When access is off, Sybyke also shows an Open Settings button the next time you use that feature.

How do I use a sticker I like?

Open the Stickers tab and tap any sticker. Choose Add to a New Page to start with that pack's template, or pick another template. The sticker is already on the page when the editor opens.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear All Data and confirm twice. This removes drafts, rendered pages, copied photos, Page Credits and the activity list from this device. Images you already saved to Photos are kept.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
